How the codebase grew, in net lines of GDScript between phase boundaries. Phase 1 (17 Oct): the first game, the orbit view and the five mayors, about 14k lines. Phase 2 (18 and 19 Oct): the whole first-person layer, built in lettered slices A to I with a spec for each, about 48k lines and 97 commits in roughly 21 hours. Phase 3 (20 to 25 Oct): 43 pull requests on top of the slices, about 46k lines. Phase 4 (26 to 30 Oct): the “Gridfall completion” mission, 98 pull requests (#47 to #145) and the web deploy, about 35k lines. This is the phase I go through next, because it is the cleanest example of running Droid at scale. Phase 5 (30 Oct to 2 Nov): hardening for the web, 25 commits and 25 new test files, about 15k lines. The totals add up to about 158k lines. These are net line differences between boundaries, not blame: refactors and deletions move lines around. Dates are IST.
