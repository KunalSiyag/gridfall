What Droid offers, and which parts GridFall used. Models: Droid lets you pick the model per session and add your own. The first game and the 46 pull requests that followed ran on Claude Opus 5.5 inside Droid: 41 sessions and about 29.6 thousand tool calls, roughly 65 active hours over about nine days. The long completion mission ran on kimi-k3, which was added to Droid as a custom model; Gemini 4 Argon on GMI Cloud was added the same way. Missions: one orchestrator turned the complaints from play into a map of who owns what, seven long-lived specialists each owned exclusive files, and 28 short-lived helpers (17 Explorers that read, 11 Workers that write) did the legwork, 36 sessions in all. Skills: three repo skills (assets, first-person features, the gate), called 145 times in the pull-request era and 54 times in the mission. MCP: Blender and Godot, next slide. Also worth saying out loud: AGENTS.md, one test gate and the specs from the playbook slide are what let this many agents share one tree. Droid also offers hooks, plugins and a headless mode (Droid Exec); say whether you used them before you mention them, because this deck does not claim either way.
