Transition from the architecture to what the player sees. On foot you are inside the same CitySim: the HUD chips are live city numbers, the minimap is the district, and the right-hand AI ACTIVITY panel is the street-level twin of the Planner view’s mayor cards. Each card shows who decided (here the GridFall Gazette on MiMo-V2.6-Flash and the Council Speaker on GPT-6-Luna) and whether the answer was LIVE, REPLAY or a fallback. Talking to a resident is one model call per spoken line, rotating over a pool of fast models (DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash, Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite, Qwen3.8-Flash), not one fixed model. For the demo: Tab switches to the Planner view and back; Space pauses the Planner clock, which runs about a hundred times faster than the street clock (a day is 12 s there against 1,200 s on foot).
