This slide is the speaker’s cue sheet; keep it up only if you want the audience to follow along. Eight beats, about 7 minutes 45 seconds, 7 minutes without the carjack. Before going live: pick ONE surface and rehearse on it (desktop gives the best picture; the web build is what people can try afterwards). Decide live or replay: live needs a GMI key pasted before the share starts (Esc, Settings, GMI Cloud, tick Remember) and never typed on screen; replay is GRIDFALL_OFFLINE=1, which uses the recorded responses. Say out loud which one it is; the cards print LIVE or REPLAY. Make a city worth showing first: a grown day with a few incidents, F5 to quick-save and F9 to reload between rehearsals. Keys: WASD walk, Shift run, E talk, J AI activity feed, ] faster, G route to the newest decision, Tab for the Planner view, Space to pause in the Planner view (its clock runs about a hundred times faster than the street’s). Things that can go wrong: the pointer is not captured in a browser tab until you click once; a live call can be slow; a fire far away is invisible from the street, so arm the disaster near the You marker. The carjack is optional and violent; the animation was reviewed in code and measured headless but nobody has watched it in pixels, so rehearse it before you decide. On the web build the warm lamp pools under street lamps are missing (Compatibility draws no decals) and tree crowns go near black at night. If the live URL is down: the 30-second trailer is the closer.
