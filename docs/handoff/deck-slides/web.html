The browser part in five steps. The export is headless and content-addressed: textures are capped (props, characters and street assets at 256 px, normal and ARM maps by kind), 92 WAV files become Ogg (15.6 MB down to 1.7 MB), and duplicate payloads are aliased. In our export of 2 November the pack went from 356.7 MB to 123.0 MB. The Docker image compresses once at build time with Brotli q11 so the 2-vCPU box never compresses anything per visitor; Caddy serves the pre-compressed files, the Brotli .pck is 87.8 MB and the .wasm 6.9 MB. A first visit moved 94.8 MB on the wire in our local headless smoke run, against 113.4 MB for the build live on 28 October. The container runs on GMI AgentBox, and the key design decision is that the static server never holds a key: each player’s browser calls api.gmi-serving.com with their own key, and without a key the game runs on the replay cache. Limits: the 58, 23 and 32 fps figures come from Droid’s measurements of the 28 October live build, not from the new image; we have not yet re-measured the new image in a real browser, and the screenshot on the right is software-rendered headless Chrome at the Low tier, so it understates how it looks on a real GPU. Also remember to say that the web night is darker on purpose and that the warm lamp pools under street lamps exist on desktop only, because the Compatibility renderer draws no decals.
