How the people were made, in four steps. First, concept art: we asked GMI Cloud's image model, hy-image-v3.5-preview, for a sheet of five armoured avatars, one per mayor model, and a turnaround sheet of the crisis landmarks, briefed as references for Blender modelling. Second, the first build: a headless Blender script (Blender 5.2, run with --background) built a stylized robot avatar and the five landmark buildings from those sheets, on the first day. Third, the look that shipped: the character slice replaced the robots with 40 realistic bodies from Microsoft Rocketbox (MIT licence; the licence file and a provenance record are committed), each with a far level of detail. A resident's body is chosen by job and gender and by a hash of the resident's id, so nurses look like nurses, builders wear hard hats and a resident looks the same in every run and after a save. Fourth, movement: Rocketbox mocap plus Quaternius animation libraries (CC0) are retargeted onto one humanoid skeleton through bone maps, so any body can play any clip. Say plainly that the concept art set the mood and the palette and the shipped people are the realistic Rocketbox bodies, which is why the caption says so. The raw sources are 2.6 GB and are never committed; one script fetches and verifies them.
