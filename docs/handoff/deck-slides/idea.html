The idea

One city, five AI mayors

Each mayor is a different
language model on GMI Cloud.
Once a game day it reads its
district, thinks in private,
posts in public and answers
with up to seven actions.

The simulation checks
every action and says
why it refused one.

The Planner view in the web build (Low tier, headless Chrome capture)

The pitch in two sentences. Every mayor runs on a different model served by GMI Cloud, and they really do behave differently: one is a utility-first planner, one a growth maximalist. Point at the screenshot: the five cards along the bottom are the five mayors, the right-hand panel is the Mayor tab with the private thought, the public post and the action list. The ring and the word You mark where the player stands in the same city. The simulation is the referee: a mayor can ask for anything, the sim validates it and the reason for a refusal is fed back into the next day’s prompt.
