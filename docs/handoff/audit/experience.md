# Lens: experience (designer's view of the player and the webinar audience)

Repo read: /home/kunalsiyag/Ambassador/Gmi_Cloud/gridfall-wt-integ, branch web/wave0, HEAD 720d4e0 (read-only). All paths below are relative to it unless absolute.
Nothing was run (no Godot, Chrome, Docker, network). Python was used only for arithmetic and for reading PNGs (PIL from ~/.venvs/gridfall-tools).
Tags: [OBS] = seen in a screenshot or read in a file:line; [INF] = derived from code/arithmetic; [UNK] = cannot be known without running or listening.
Scores in the structured result: showcase_value / stability_value are 1-10 (10 best); stability_value = how much it lowers the chance of a bad live moment or leaves the build safer.

## 0. Verdict in five lines
1. The street HUD, activity feed, beacons, overview key bar and the Planner mayor cards are good, and they carry the showcase story (person first, model badge second, LIVE/REPLAY tag, thought/post/actions). Keep them.
2. The first 60 seconds are the weakest part: day 1 starts at 06:00 in the dark, in rain (web, seed 7), facing a wall, with pop 0, and the only guidance is one truncated toast.
3. A fresh city has long dead stretches (first AI card after 108 s at 1x, nobody to talk to, no incident before day 4); the run sheet only works from a grown save, and the web build cannot ship one.
4. The cues a presenter needs (force a mayor turn, spark an incident near me, ask the five mayors one question, skip to dusk) are either hidden (T flush, /mayor), orbit-only (Disasters tab) or missing.
5. Legibility and accessibility for a streamed demo are thin: no UI scale, 12-14 px feed text, faint speed readout, no reduce-motion, no colour-blind option, no mute key on foot.

## 2. Demo loop: pacing arithmetic (street clock = 1200 s per day, scripts/fp/world_scale.gd:108; staggered turns SPAN 0.9, scripts/ai/mayor_turns.gd:9,90-91)

The order rotates daily; Day 1 starts with Viktor Han (matches "Next up: Viktor Han in 2 min" in the screenshots).

## 4. Accessibility and input
- [OBS] Keys use physical keycodes (54 physical, 0 logical in project.godot), so WASD survives AZERTY; arrows are bound; 35 of 59 actions have gamepad bindings but none of J (feed), T, F1, F5, F9, Enter (chat), K, L, B (phone) and the legend shows keyboard caps only. No rebinding UI (Controls tab = sensitivity + invert).
- [OBS] No subtitles needed (no voiced lines found), but also no reduce-motion: only a Head bob toggle (pause_menu.gd:41); hit-stop and camera shake are in hitfeel_feature.gd (:24-34) with no setting; banner blinks twice (hud_toasts.gd:18-19); toasts slide; the web loader honours prefers-reduced-motion (web_export.py:401) but the game never reads it (grep).
- [INF, computed] Mayor colours #FFB800 / #00F0FF / #B44CFF / #10E88A / #FF2E54 (scripts/sim/sim_defs.gd:218-238) under Machado 2009 severity 1.0 simulation, CIE76 dE between pairs (smallest first): deuteranopia Rosa(green) vs Duke(red) 21.1, then Ada vs Duke 44.9; protanopia Ada(amber) vs Rosa(green) 36.2; tritanopia Viktor(cyan) vs Rosa(green) 10.9. Normal vision minimum 59.7. A dE under ~25 on small swatches is weak. Mitigation present: colour is always paired with the person's name in cards, tags, chat; the district fill on the minimap is 2% (minimap_model.gd:232), so it is not relied on. Colour-only uses to check: the world beacons and the Planner district outlines/labels (city_view.gd:1652,1692,1819,3102).
- [OBS] 29 of the 86 flags in data/feature_flags.json are about combat, grapples, hit feel and carjacks (by my reading of the names); none is about presenter, onboarding, accessibility or scale. Not a defect by itself; it shows where the design attention went versus the stated intent (showcase of different models interacting). Left click throws a punch once the pointer is captured, so a presenter's stray click on stage is a punch (the first capturing click is consumed, fp_mode.gd:370-373).
