About 1 minute. Keep it simple: this is a city simulator, like SimCity, except you do not run it. Five AI mayors do. Each mayor is a different AI model hosted on GMI Cloud, so they really do make different decisions. Left picture: the view from above, which the game calls the Planner view. The five cards along the bottom are the five mayors, and the red banner at the top is the Chaos Director (another AI) bursting a sewage pipe. Right picture: the same city on foot, in first person. The panel on the right shows which AI decided what. The two views are not two cities. It is one city, and you press Tab to switch.
