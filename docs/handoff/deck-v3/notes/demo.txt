About 3 minutes live. Before you start: decide live or replay. Live needs your GMI key pasted in Settings before you share the screen, and never typed on screen. Replay (offline) uses the recorded answers and always works. Watch: press Tab to be above the city, speed up the days with the right-bracket key, open a mayor card and read its private thought and public post. Walk: Tab back to the street, walk to a resident, press E and ask about the mayors. Every spoken line is one model call. Break it: open the Disasters tab, arm a sewage main burst and click the map. Then walk toward it and watch the residents and the news react. If anything misbehaves, say so and move on; the point of the game is that the world is not scripted.
