About 1 minute 30. This is the character story, told in three pictures. One, the sketch: before any 3D work I asked GMI's image model, hy-image-v3.5-preview, for a concept sheet of the five mayors as armoured characters. That gave us a look and a colour for each mayor: gold, blue, violet, green and red. Two, the model: a script in Blender, running with no window, built a simple robot from that idea, plus the crisis landmark buildings, which were sketched the same way. This picture is a fresh Blender render of that robot, a stand-in that proved the pipeline. Three, the game: for the street view we wanted real people, so the robots were replaced by forty realistic bodies (Microsoft Rocketbox, free to use) with animations from Quaternius, a free animation pack. A nurse looks like a nurse and a builder wears a hard hat, and a resident always gets the same body, even after you save and reload. This last picture is the real player character in the game. Be plain about it: the image model gave us the sketches and the direction, Blender turned them into the first models, and the final people come from free character packs.
