About 1 minute 30. Introduce the cast by person first, model second. Ada Okafor is run by Argon-4 and plans around utilities. Viktor Han runs on DeepSeek-V4 and thinks like a systems engineer. Mei Kimura runs on Kimi-K3 and builds coalitions. Rosa Quintero runs on Qwen-3.8 and watches the budget. Duke Granger runs on Grok-4.5 and wants growth. Once a game day each mayor looks at its district, thinks in private, posts a public message and asks for up to seven actions. The city checks every action and says why it refused one. That is the point of the project: the same city, five different models, and nobody can predict what happens. Around the mayors there are five more AI helpers (a newspaper, a council speaker, a disaster director, a power-grid operator and a street inspector), and every resident has a mind of their own. Say where the pictures came from: they are concept art drawn by GMI's image model, which comes back later in the story.
