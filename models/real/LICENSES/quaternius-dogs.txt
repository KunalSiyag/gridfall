Changes for GridFall: the duplicate "AnimalArmature|<clip>" animations were dropped (the plain
clips are kept), the looping clips (Walk, Gallop, Idle, Idle_2, Idle_2_HeadLow, Eating) carry the
"-loop" suffix Godot's importer turns into a looping animation, and the flat materials are matte
(metallic 0, roughness 0.9 for the coat, 0.35 for the eyes and nose). Geometry, skeleton and
keyframes are unchanged.
