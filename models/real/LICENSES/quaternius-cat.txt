GridFall cat model - third-party content

Model by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com), licensed CC0 1.0 (public domain,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No attribution is required; it is listed
here for provenance. From "Animal Pack Vol.2 by @Quaternius" (Blends/Cat.blend), fetched from
OpenGameArt:

  Cat  https://opengameart.org/content/animated-animales-low-poly  -> animals/cat.glb

Changes for GridFall (tools/blender/make_cat.py): the bones are renamed, the source's Idle and
Walking clips are baked to plain bone keys and its leg IK dropped, the mesh is smoothed once, the
coat fields the game's cat shader reads are written into UV, UV2 and the vertex colours, the
other clips (Trot, Run, Stalk, Crouch, Pounce, Sit, Loaf, Groom, Sleep, Hiss, Startle, Stretch)
are posed in code, and looping clips carry the "-loop" suffix Godot's importer turns into a loop.
